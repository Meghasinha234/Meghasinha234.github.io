01 / ABOUT

Designing for failure.

I am a backend software engineer focused on what happens when distributed systems break. I spent two years at Oracle Cloud Infrastructure engineering asynchronous workflows for subscription lifecycles across commercial, government, and sovereign cloud realms.

My approach to engineering is defensive: I build to prevent silent failures, eliminate redundant request fanouts, and automate recovery pipelines. I prefer dealing with idempotency, bounded retries, and race conditions over building simple CRUD apps.
